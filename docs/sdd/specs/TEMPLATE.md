## Problem and goal

Who is affected, what problem exists today, and what outcome is expected? Cite the source of the requirement. Avoid describing the technical solution as the goal.

## Scope

**In scope:** behaviors this change delivers.  
**Out of scope:** boundaries that matter for review.

## Expected behavior

Describe inputs, actions, and observable outcomes for the main flow. Include empty, loading, error, retry, and recovery states when relevant. For UI, include navigation, essential copy, and accessibility. For integrations, state the known contract and handling of unavailability without including secrets.
